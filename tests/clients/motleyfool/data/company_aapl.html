About Apple

Apple makes smartphones and computers that help people connect, work, and learn. Its iPhone and Mac products are used by individuals, businesses, and schools. Apple’s services, like Apple Music and iCloud, help users store data and access entertainment easily.

Gadgets, Gizmos, and More: Apple’s Core Offerings

  • iPhone (47.4% of net sales in Q3 FY2025): Smartphones for communication, work, and entertainment; used by individuals and businesses.
  • Services represented approximately 29.2% of Apple’s total net sales in Q3 FY2025.: Includes Apple Music, iCloud, App Store, and Apple TV+; provides digital content and cloud storage.
  • Mac represented approximately 8.6% of Apple’s total net sales in Q3 FY2025.: Laptops and desktop computers for personal, educational, and professional use.
  • iPad represented approximately 7.0% of Apple’s total net sales in Q3 FY2025.: Tablets for reading, drawing, and productivity; popular in education and business.
  • Wearables, Home, and Accessories represented approximately 7.9% of Apple’s total net sales in Q3 FY2025.: Includes Apple Watch, AirPods, and smart home devices for health, audio, and convenience.

From Garage to Global: Apple’s Expansion Playbook

  • Expanded services with Apple Music, Apple TV+, and Apple Arcade.
  • Ongoing partnerships with major telecom carriers worldwide and providers of co-branded credit card services such as Apple Card.

Why Apple’s Still in the Game

Apple’s integrated ecosystem links devices and services for a seamless user experience. Its global distribution network reaches consumers in many regions. Regular software updates and strong brand loyalty support its competitive position.

Five Things You Need to Know

  • Revenue was $143.76 billion in QQ1 2026, up 15.7% year-over-year.
  • Services segment grew 13% year-over-year in Q3 2025.
  • Operating cash flow was $53.92 billion in QQ1 2026, up 80.1% year-over-year.
  • Americas accounted for 43.8% of Q3 2025 sales; Greater China 16.4%.

Bottom Line

Apple has evolved from a computer maker to a diversified technology company. Its mix of hardware and services supports steady growth and adaptability.
